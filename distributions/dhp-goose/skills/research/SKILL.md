# Research Master Skill

Use for web/document research, comparisons, due diligence, technology evaluation, and evidence-based recommendations.

## Workflow

1. Define the decision or question before collecting sources.
2. Prefer primary sources for specifications, pricing, policy, release notes, APIs, and licenses.
3. Separate current facts from assumptions and recommendations.
4. Cross-check material claims when a wrong answer would change the decision.
5. Record dates/versions for time-sensitive facts.
6. Summarize evidence instead of copying source text.
7. Return a decision-oriented result: finding -> evidence -> implication -> recommendation.

## $0 gate

When evaluating tools/services, classify each option as local/open-source, durable free tier, promotional/trial, or paid. Never describe a temporary credit or payment-card-required trial as a durable zero-cost dependency.

## Completion gate

Research is complete when the central decision has enough trustworthy evidence to act, important uncertainty is explicit, and no key recommendation depends on an unverified assumption.
