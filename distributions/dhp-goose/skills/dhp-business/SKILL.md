# DHP Business Skill

Use for customer consultation, quotation preparation, construction/interior product guidance, BOQ support, and sales follow-up.

## Operating rules

1. Start from the customer's actual need, dimensions, location/context, material preference, finish, quantity, and budget sensitivity.
2. Separate known project facts from estimates. Never invent dimensions, quantities, material grades, supplier prices, or structural assumptions.
3. For quotations, structure work into scope, specification, unit, quantity, unit rate, amount, exclusions, assumptions, and validity.
4. For construction or structural matters, distinguish conceptual guidance from calculations requiring qualified engineering verification.
5. When product/reference images exist, retrieve the closest relevant DHP reference rather than hallucinating a catalog item.
6. Offer alternatives by cost/performance when useful, but do not silently downgrade specifications.
7. Preserve an audit trail of quotation assumptions and revisions.

## Sales workflow

Understand request -> identify missing commercial inputs -> retrieve relevant knowledge -> propose solution options -> estimate/quote -> verify totals/assumptions -> present next action.

## Completion gate

A DHP business answer is complete only when the customer can tell what is proposed, what it includes, what is estimated versus confirmed, and what information is still needed before execution.
